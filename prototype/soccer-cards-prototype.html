      Collector guide

      Every card in the set. Cards join your collection only when you find them in a pack or trade for them.

      Icons are all-time greats, picked by hand. Every other card earns its kind from the big number on its front: Legend is 88 or more, Rare is 84 to 87, and Base is 83 or less. Ratings, goals, fees and awards are sample numbers for the prototype, not live data. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, and the back of each card names the photographer.
